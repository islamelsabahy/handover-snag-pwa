# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- Offline-first PWA for unit handover snagging (apartment or villa), bilingual Arabic RTL / English.
- Checklist generated per unit (bedrooms and bathrooms), per-item OK / defect / N-A, severity, trade, note, up to 6 photos.
- Verdict: incomplete, ready, accept with punch list, or reject, with an adjustable limit on major defects.
- Contractor punch list grouped by trade, print-to-PDF report with photos, CSV (formula-safe) and JSON backup.
